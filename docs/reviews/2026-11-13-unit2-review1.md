1. **P2 — Different keys still render identically without IDs.** `desktop/src/rowFacts.ts:69` compares separate raw strings rather than their rendered composition.

   **Reproduced:** otherwise identical keys with issuers `Acme  Cloud` and `Acme Cloud` draw identically. Their accessible descriptions also normalize identically. Another collision occurs between issuer `Acme · 1 scope` with no scopes and issuer `Acme` with one scope. Neither pair receives IDs. Headless Edge reproduced both with matching key glyphs at 900 px in both themes, with forced colours enabled and disabled.

   **Closing test:** guard these pairs with matching glyphs and require distinct visible IDs and accessible identities in all four modes.

2. **P2 — Live-key confirmations discard the facts that distinguish their targets.** `desktop/src/components/FactList.tsx:25` omits expiry and renewal, while the row comparison uses them to decide that IDs are unnecessary.

   **Reproduced:** two keys sharing their identity fields, differing only by expiry, have distinguishable rows but identical move-to-Recycle-Bin confirmations, including their accessible descriptions. Renewal-only differences reproduce the same problem. Rotation uses the same reduced facts; its dialog also has no accessible target description.

   **Closing test:** open each target's move and rotation confirmation when only expiry or renewal differs. Require distinguishing visible and accessible facts, and verify that confirmation sends the selected ID.

3. **P2 — A recovered terminal outcome leaves Use keys permanently busy.** `desktop/src/components/UseKeys.tsx:214` clears its busy state only when the launch promise finishes.

   **Reproduced in Edge:** hold the reply indefinitely, then deliver `terminalStarted`, `terminalFailed` or `terminalDeclined` through warnings. The global receipt settles and acknowledges the record, but the launch button and export controls remain disabled. The page still instructs the user to answer the confirmation window.

   **Closing test:** keep the reply unresolved, settle each outcome from its record, and require the originating form to stop waiting. An older operation's record must not clear a newer operation's busy state.

4. **P2 — An obsolete plaintext preview reappears after import and read recovery.** `desktop/src/components/UseKeys.tsx:122` binds the preview only to project and environment.

   **Reproduced with mocked IPC:** preview a scope, import a replacement value into it, fail the post-import list read, then retry successfully. The pre-import plaintext disappears during failure and returns after retry, without another export-preview request.

   **Closing test:** exercise that sequence and a preview reply delayed across import. Obsolete plaintext must remain cleared until a fresh preview succeeds.

5. **P3 — Aliased reads bypass the read guard.** `desktop/src/readGuard.test.ts:31` recognizes the spelling `api`, not its imported binding.

   **Reproduced:** injecting a component containing `import { api as native } … native.keys().then(setKeys)` leaves all six guard tests passing. Helper and dynamic-alias forms also escape the scanner.

   **Closing test:** require guard failures for aliased imports, helper exports, dynamic access and effects in hook files.

6. **P3 — Approved wording can conceal an additional unsupported promise.** `desktop/src/promiseWords.test.ts:40` accepts an entire text when it contains an approved substring.

   **Reproduced:** appending "This app guarantees that your secrets are always safe" to the approved Recycle Bin sentence leaves all three guard tests passing.

   **Closing test:** combine approved wording with a second unsupported promise and require rejection of the additional promise.

7. **P3 — Acknowledgement depends on an unstated error-provenance contract.** `desktop/src/api.ts:67` treats every rejection with a `kind` as a native reply and acknowledges its record.

   **Inspected and demonstrated with a synthetic rejection:** `{ kind: "transport", … }` causes acknowledgement. The written contract does not establish that transport failures lack `kind`. Actual Tauri reachability of that rejection shape was **not established**, so this is a contract/proof gap.

   **Closing test:** define error provenance at the boundary and prove that transport failures preserve the outcome record through reload.

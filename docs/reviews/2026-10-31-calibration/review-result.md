## Entry verdict

The findings below matter for the first implementation unit and prevent claims that journal outcomes or privacy are complete. They do not justify real-secret use, installation, publication, or an above-90 claim.

## Ranked findings

1. **P2, inspection-supported current defect: warnings can be attributed to the wrong vault session.** `desktop/src-tauri/src/session.rs:72,160-167,232-252,313-319,364-386,495-499` keeps one unowned `Gate.warnings` queue across lock and subsequent opens. `desktop/src/App.tsx:31-43,88-102,213-220` clears only the displayed warnings and polls every three seconds. If A commits while its audit append fails, then the user locks and opens B before polling, B can drain A's "vault saved, but the audit log..." warning. A can also lose its warning to B when locking overtakes the commit. The earlier frontend test for a late A warning *reply* is not a test of this native queue. Bind warnings to the originating operation/session, preserve a secret-free acknowledgment when A is gone, and never present A's warning as B's. Add native plus real App/API interleaving regressions for both ordinary and `completedWhileLocked` cases.

2. **P2, inspection-supported current defect: journal presentation overstates both verification and completed events.** `audit.rs:104-107,128-154` returns a successful verification for a nonexistent log. `Activity.tsx:52-54,73-83,118-124` says every action is recorded, displays "Chain verified", and can say nothing happened. Yet `Vault::create`/`save` at `vault.rs:42-75,453-455` do not add an init entry, and post-commit logging can fail. Further, `commands.rs:203-221,359-367` logs reveal/copy/export before the later disclosure or clipboard operation, so cancellation or failure can leave an event shown as completed. `commands.rs:279-282` verifies and reads under separate locks, so a concurrent append can cause displayed rows to differ from the verified snapshot. `akm audit --verify` also reports verification for a missing file at `main.rs:421-434`. Use explicit absent, corrupt, consistent-but-unauthenticated and incomplete/unknown states; distinguish attempts from known outcomes; read and verify one locked snapshot. Test missing/deleted logs, failed clipboard or lock after logging, failed post-commit append, and an interleaved append. No unanchored chain can establish completeness or identify who changed it.

3. **P2, design gap: identity and permissions need a concrete migration contract.** `PHASE2.md:23-36` identifies the decisions but does not settle them. `Vault::open` uses a path (`vault.rs:77-95`), every successful replacement can change file identity (`vault.rs:514-524`), and `akm audit` currently operates on a path without unlocking (`main.rs:421-422`). A path-only journal splits on rename and aliases a replacement; a content hash changes on save; a KDBX root UUID is copied with a clone. Decide how the per-device mapping handles rename, clone, replacement, rollback, two separate vaults, unknown/ambiguous identity, and audit without a password. Do not silently write old KDBX files, merge clone histories, or fall back to the synced sidecar. Specify a non-roaming local location, secure creation and verification of Windows ACLs for journal, lock and mapping, and safe behavior for reparse points and unsupported storage. Acceptance must cover concurrent mapping/append, denied permissions, older-format and filesystem-read-only vaults, and legacy sidecars without deleting, importing or promising removal of already synced copies.

4. **P2, design gap: tail damage does not reveal its cause, and pre-disclosure failures differ from post-commit failures.** `PHASE2.md:27-30` asks for malformed-tail handling. `audit.rs:181-196` currently rejects malformed JSON, but even a truncated last record cannot prove whether a crash or deliberate alteration caused it. Preserve original bytes, classify observable damage without asserting cause, and never silently bless an altered prefix. Test truncated final JSON, valid JSON with broken links, damaged middle records, missing logs and concurrent reads. Separately, the current CLI intentionally refuses reveal, export and launch when pre-disclosure logging fails (`crates/akm-cli/tests/security.rs:435-461`; `crates/akm-cli/README.md:28-33`); native reveal/copy also log first. Preserve that fail-closed behavior unless an explicit reviewed policy change replaces it. A failure *after* a committed vault write or published export must instead yield a truthful, non-retryable success and warning.

5. **P2, design risk: native history and deletion require more than selecting a library API.** `vault.rs:221-260,341-372` currently edits or removes without tracking history or tombstones. In pinned `keepass` 0.17.0, `EntryMut::remove` removes the entry, whereas `EntryTrack::remove` adds a deleted-object record (`src/db/types/entry.rs:606-662,730-739`); a tracked move updates location time (`:723-727`). Its `EntryTrack` adds history on drop even if nothing changed (`:843-851`), and KDBX time has second precision (`src/db/types/times.rs:3-6`). Before implementation define no-op detection, rapid same-second edits, clock rollback, movement into a recycle bin versus permanent deletion, restore parent/UUID, and tombstone timing. Test both merge directions with stale foreign copies and actual KeePassXC, including disabled/missing recycle bin, existing foreign history, shared and history-only attachments. The plan already requires these broad properties; these are concrete acceptance cases, not a claim that its future design is broken.

6. **P2, inspection-supported current defect: expiry and rotation status can become stale while the Keys view remains open.** `views.rs:58-80` calculates `expired` and `rotation_due_in` when the key list is fetched. `KeysView.tsx:32-46` fetches that list on mount, and `format.ts:47-56` trusts the stored `expired` and rotation values. The status poll in `App.tsx:88-102,223-224` does not refresh the key list. If an entry expires while that view stays mounted, it can continue to show "Expires soon" rather than "Expired"; rotation can likewise retain its old day count. Compute or refresh time-derived statuses according to the chosen clock policy without making polling count as user activity. Test crossing expiry and rotation boundaries while mounted, including hidden/resumed and clock-change cases.

7. **P3, inspection-supported evidence limitation: the earlier GUI proof is reported but not independently identified in the consolidated record I found.** `PHASE1.md:123,142-150` and `docs/history/phase1-final/evidence.json:35-48` distinguish earlier import/clone/upgrade checks from final `-RemainingOnly`, but give only the final GUI hash and a summary of the prior run, not the earlier GUI hash or its raw native-run output. Thus a fresh agent can recover the reported scope but cannot independently tie that prior proof to an exact GUI binary from those records alone. If surviving evidence establishes it, archive the identity and output with provenance; otherwise explicitly mark them unavailable. Do not relabel them as final-executable checks or rerun them merely to fill this review.

8. **P3, remaining correctness and accessibility work is real, not a reason to redo fixed findings.** `model.rs:80-85` derives rotation from modification time and truncates whole days, so a key less than one day overdue can still show due today. `main.rs:539-549` uses midnight UTC while `KeyForm.tsx:92` uses end-of-day UTC. `dotenv.rs:46-75,81-114` accepts `TOKEN=abc # note` with the comment in the value. `Vault::find` and `ApiKey::matches` (`vault.rs:173-195`, `model.rs:54-74`) allow a unique notes/URL match for both `get --reveal` and `rm --yes` (`main.rs:297-305,320-342`). `ApiKey`/`ApiKeyInput` derive secret-bearing `Debug`, and list constructs secrets (`model.rs:31-36,95-99`, `vault.rs:151-163`). `KeysView.tsx:260-265,289-292` overrides row text with a title-only accessible name. Pin a non-interpolating external dotenv profile, use literal cross-zone/expiry and overdue boundary fixtures, decide exact-only or explicit fuzzy confirmation for destructive/disclosure commands, and test metadata-only/Debug and accessible status. M13 notification content must remain opt-in and safe while locked; native delivery needs its own evidence.
